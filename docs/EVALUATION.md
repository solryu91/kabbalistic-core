# Public evaluation harness

## What the harness measures

The paired evaluation compares two response pipelines under one intention,
retrieval snapshot, model configuration, call count, and output-token ceiling:

- the graph treatment, including Form, Flow, Accord, directed observation,
  Tiferet integration, and optional bounded symbolic structure; and
- a neutral evidence/alternatives/consequences pipeline without the Tree route,
  functional kernels, or symbolic recursion.

The code creates a balanced A/B packet, keeps the condition key separate,
requires every rubric score, hash-seals the score sheet before unblinding, and
applies a preregistered decision rule. Small or mixed differences remain null
instead of being relabeled after the fact.

## Public-edition boundary

The `preregistered_v0.1.json` through `v0.3.json` files are project-authored
`synthetic_public_fixture` inputs. They preserve the public evaluation API,
blinding, sealing, decision-rule behavior, and protocol evolution for tests and
new local experiments.

This edition contains no results or summaries derived from nonpublic research
runs. It also excludes private corpora, paired outputs, evaluator packets,
condition keys, score sheets, source locators, and private Git history.

## Interpretation limits

Synthetic fixtures and small local runs do not support statistical
generalization. They cannot establish consciousness, personhood, relationship
development, metaphysical truth, product efficacy, or general architectural
superiority. Future work should use independently reviewed public prompts,
multiple evaluators, larger samples, calibrated measures, and a preregistration
frozen before outcomes are generated.
